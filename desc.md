**Pay with your card while borrowing against your XRP: spend privately, with no credit check, inside a TEE nobody can peek into, then turn your card debt into a standard XLS-66 loan on XRPL.**

**The problem.** Holding XRP and spending it are at odds today. To pay for something with your XRP you have to sell it, give up your exposure and create a taxable event, or hand it to a custodian and trust them with your funds and your data. Traditional credit cards need a credit check and a bank relationship, and they see every purchase you make. On-chain lending is transparent but slow, and putting every card payment on a public ledger would publish your whole life.

**What was built.** An XRP-backed credit card that combines the speed and privacy of a card network with the settlement and transparency of XRPL. You deposit XRP as collateral to a deposit account, and your credit limit is simply your collateral. No credit check, no bank account, no custodian holding your keys, and nobody sees your purchases.

When you pay, the card network asks the Credit Server for approval. The Credit Server is a Flare Confidential Compute extension running inside a trusted execution environment (TEE). It checks your position, locks enough XRP collateral, books the purchase as RLUSD debt and approves in well under a second, fast enough for a real card terminal. All of this happens inside the TEE. Nobody sees your individual purchases, not even the machine's operator: not the amounts, the merchants or the times.

**Settlement on XRPL.** Individual payments never touch the chain. At each settlement the TEE closes the card spend since the last one, and the pool pays one RLUSD payment on XRPL to the settlement address for all merchants together. The ledger sees one aggregate number per settlement. That is enough for full accountability, reveals nothing about any cardholder, and still settles according to the rules of the payment network.

**From card debt to a real loan.** When you want to free your XRP without paying in full, you can convert part or all of your debt into a standard XLS-66 loan on XRPL. That loan works just like the loan a card issuer offers on a credit card balance: you pay it off in fixed monthly installments. The Credit Server opens a repayment account for you, a 1-of-2 multisig of your key and the Credit Server's. It takes the loan and repays your card debt to the pool under your tag, and the TEE unlocks the matching collateral. The loan itself is public and lives entirely on XRPL, and you repay it from your own wallet. It's the bridge from a private, collateralized credit line to an ordinary on-chain loan, underwritten the way a bank would underwrite it.

**A real lending market.** Card spending is funded by one lending pool inside the TEE. Anyone can be a liquidity provider: deposit RLUSD from the Liquidity provider app and receive XCLP pool tokens on XRPL. As cardholders pay their 12% interest, XCLP grows in value, and the app shows your APY and your earnings ticking up every minute. The operator supplies liquidity on exactly the same terms as everyone else. LPs can opt in now to the next round, an uncollateralized lending protocol with a higher APY: a slider marks part of their deposit for it, saved off chain with nothing signed until the round opens.

**Your keys, your choice.** Every transaction can be signed with Xaman or a Ledger hardware wallet. With Ledger, every collateral deposit, trust line, loan installment and LP deposit is confirmed on the device, and the key never leaves it.

**Why the TEE matters.** Without a TEE, whoever runs the card server effectively is the bank: they can read every purchase, raise one user's limit or quietly drain the pool. With Flare Confidential Compute, the code is the policy. The Credit Server's image builds reproducibly, so anyone can rebuild it and compare its hash with the one registered on chain. A machine only goes live after it proves it runs that exact code and passes the data providers' availability check. Every result it signs can be verified by smart contracts. The fast path, approving at the terminal, runs as a direct action; settlement goes through signed on-chain instructions.

**Built on XRPL's strengths.** Destination tags give per-user accounting with no per-user accounts. RLUSD gives a stablecoin to settle in. Multisig repayment accounts need no smart contract, and XLS-66 lending vaults model how credit is actually funded. Ledgers close in seconds for fractions of a cent, so the whole flow runs live on XRPL Devnet: deposits, settlement, loans and LP shares.

**What's next.** The XRPL keys move into the TEE as wallet keys. And the Credit Server runs on AWS SEV-SNP instances, where AMD silicon proves the code, AWS's certificates and compliance (PCI DSS among them) vouch for the infrastructure, and Flare's registry enforces which code may act. That combination is what card issuers and banks already trust.

The speed and privacy of a card network, the transparency of XRPL, and trust anyone can verify: fully non-custodial and secure.
