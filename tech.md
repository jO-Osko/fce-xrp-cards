The Credit Server is a Flare Confidential Compute extension (Go) running in a TEE. It keeps a confidential ledger of XRP collateral, RLUSD card debt (interest accruing continuously) and an ERC-4626-style lending pool for perpetual loans. Card payments are authorized in under a second as direct actions; daily settlement runs as signed on-chain instructions. A Node watcher credits tagged XRPL Devnet payments to the TEE, pays merchant settlement and LP payouts in RLUSD, and issues XCLP pool shares as a trust-line token. Card debt converts to XLS-66 loans from a closed-ended vault through a 1-of-2 multisig repayment account and a mock loan broker. The Next.js app has cardholder, LP and operator views. Users sign with Xaman or a Ledger (WebHID), with each transaction prepared by the watcher. All XRPL traffic goes over one connection. Every transaction carries a configured SourceTag.
