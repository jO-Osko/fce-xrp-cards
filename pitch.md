# The pitch

**One line:** a credit card backed by your XRP, approved in under a second by code nobody can peek into, settled daily in RLUSD on XRPL.

## Focus on three moments

1. **Swipe.** Deposit XRP as collateral, tap the Mock Card: approved in under a second, inside a TEE. Nobody, not even the operator, sees what you bought. Only the daily total ever touches the chain.
2. **Settle.** One RLUSD payment on XRPL pays every merchant for the day. The 1% card fee goes to the treasury in the same run. Show the transaction on the explorer.
3. **Earn.** Switch to the Liquidity provider app: the APY in big numbers, a Ledger confirming the deposit, and earnings ticking up by the minute, funded by cardholders' interest.

## Say this

- **No credit check.** Your limit is your collateral (75% LTV), so anyone with XRP gets a card.
- **Private by design.** Card activity stays inside Flare Confidential Compute. The code is the policy: anyone can rebuild it and check its hash on chain.
- **Real XRPL rails.** Destination tags, RLUSD, XLS-66 loans: card debt can turn into a regular on-chain loan, which frees your XRP.
- **A real market.** Cardholders pay 12%, LPs earn it, and the next round adds uncollateralized lending for a higher APY.
- **Next: ready for banks.** On AWS SEV-SNP, AMD would prove the code and AWS's certificates and compliance the infrastructure, the combination issuers already trust ([amazon.md](amazon.md)).

## Close

The speed and privacy of a card network, the transparency of XRPL, and trust anyone can verify.
